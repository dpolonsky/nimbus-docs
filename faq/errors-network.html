What does error E42 mean and how does it relate to firmware 3.x?

E42 is a pairing key mismatch. It follows a 3.x update because the update resets the unit key while the app keeps the old one. Fix it by re-enabling pairing, then factory reset if it persists.

What does error E17 mean?

The update stopped because charge fell below 50 percent or power was lost. Connect the supplied USB-C adapter and retry.

Source: E17, Steps

Why does a factory reset clear E42?

Removal clears the key in the app and the reset clears it on the unit, so both negotiate a new one.

My unit drops off Wi-Fi every few minutes. What should I try?

Move it away from electronics and microwaves, set the router 2.4 GHz channel to 1, 6 or 11, and restart router then unit. A factory reset will not help.

Source: Common steps

Why can’t my X100 see my Wi-Fi network?

The X100 supports 2.4 GHz only. A router broadcasting only on 5 GHz is invisible to it.

Source: Router on 5 GHz only

What does a factory reset erase?

Saved Wi-Fi networks, the pairing key and local settings. Cloud account data is kept.
