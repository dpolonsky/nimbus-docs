FAQ: Pairing

Product: All · Firmware: - · Status: published · Updated: 2026-11-09

Nimbus Docs › Frequently Asked Questions › FAQ: Pairing › Do I need to pair my X100 again after a firmware update?

Do I need to pair my X100 again after a firmware update?

No. The X100 keeps the key. See X100 firmware notes

Nimbus Docs › Frequently Asked Questions › FAQ: Pairing › Do I need to pair my X200 again after the 3.x update?

Do I need to pair my X200 again after the 3.x update?

Yes, the update resets the key. See E42

Nimbus Docs › Frequently Asked Questions › FAQ: Pairing › How long do I have to confirm the pairing code?

How long do I have to confirm the pairing code?

60 seconds. See Re-enable Pairing Mode

Nimbus Docs › Frequently Asked Questions › FAQ: Pairing › Does the X500 need re-pairing after updates?

Does the X500 need re-pairing after updates?

No. The key rotates silently. See X500 firmware notes

Nimbus Docs › Frequently Asked Questions › FAQ: Pairing › Why does my X300 show Cloud Offline after an update?

Why does my X300 show Cloud Offline after an update?

The 4.x update re-issues the cloud token. See E61

Nimbus Docs › Frequently Asked Questions › FAQ: Pairing › Why did my X400 ask me to pair again once?

Why did my X400 ask me to pair again once?

Update from 4.0 to 4.1 resets the key once. See E43
