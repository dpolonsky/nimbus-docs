My X200 won’t pair with the app after the update. What do I do?

The 3.x update resets the pairing key, so the app and the unit disagree. Re-enable pairing mode in the app and pair again. If error E42 persists after two attempts, remove the unit in the app, factory reset it and add it as new.

Source: Pairing (X200), Steps

Why is my X200 Offline while the LED is steady white?

White steady means the unit is ready, so the radio is fine. Offline after an update means the pairing key was reset and the app needs to pair again.

Source: Pairing (X200), Cause

Nimbus Docs › Frequently Asked Questions › FAQ: Pairing › X200 pairing › How long do I have to confirm the pairing code?

How long do I have to confirm the pairing code?

60 seconds from the moment pairing mode starts. If it times out, start again.

Source: Re-enable Pairing Mode, Steps

Where do I start pairing on an X100?

On the unit itself: Settings → Bluetooth → Pair new device. The app then confirms it.

Source: X100 pairing flow

Nimbus Docs › Frequently Asked Questions › FAQ: Pairing › X100 pairing › Do I need to pair my X100 again after a firmware update?

Do I need to pair my X100 again after a firmware update?

No. Firmware 2.x keeps the pairing key. If you have to pair again it is a fault: forget the device on both sides and pair again.

Source: X100 key preserved

Nimbus Docs › Frequently Asked Questions › FAQ: Pairing › General › How do I know which pairing guide to use?

How do I know which pairing guide to use?

Check the firmware in About: 2.x is an X100, 3.x is an X200. If the unit is Offline, grey base means X100 and white base means X200.

Source: Identify your unit

Nimbus Docs › Frequently Asked Questions › FAQ: Pairing › General › What is a pairing key?

What is a pairing key?

A secret shared by the unit and the app. If the two copies differ the unit is rejected.

Source: Glossary, Pairing key
