Why does the X200 forget its pairing after every update?

Firmware 3.x generates a new key on each update for security. The app cannot receive it, so the unit must be paired again. Since 3.2 the app warns before it happens.

Can I roll my X200 back to 2.x?

No. Rollback is not supported because 3.x changes the pairing and storage format.

Source: Firmware Rollback

How long does an X200 update take?

Up to 10 minutes. The LED is amber; do not unplug the unit.

Source: The update process

What charge does an update need?

At least 50 percent, or a connected USB-C supply. Otherwise the update stops with E17.

Source: Update prerequisites

What changed in X100 firmware 2.3?

It added Wi-Fi channel auto-selection.
