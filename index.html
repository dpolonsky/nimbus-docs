Product: All · Firmware: - · Status: published · Updated: 2026-10-25

This site is the service and troubleshooting reference for the Nimbus X-series devices. Procedures differ by model and firmware, so always identify your unit before following any steps.

Which unit do I have?

  1. Look at the label on the underside of the unit. X100 units have a grey base; X200 units have a white base.
  2. In the Nimbus app open Devices → your unit → About and read Firmware. 2.x means X100 firmware; 3.x means X200 firmware.

Most common problems

  • The unit will not pair after a firmware update: see Pairing (X200) or Pairing (X100).
  • The app shows error E42 or E17: see Error Codes.
  • Connection drops every few minutes: see Wi-Fi Dropouts.
