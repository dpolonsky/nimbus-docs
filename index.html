Nimbus Docs › What this site covers

What this site covers

This is the service and troubleshooting reference for the Nimbus X-series devices (X100 and X200). It is organised in three parts: Products (what each model is and how it behaves), Troubleshooting (symptoms, error codes and recovery procedures) and a FAQ that answers the most common questions with links back to the exact section that backs each answer.

Procedures differ by model and firmware. A pairing procedure written for the X100 does not work on the X200, and the other way round. Always identify the unit first.

Nimbus Docs › Identify your unit

Identify your unit

Use either method. The app method is the most reliable.

Nimbus Docs › Identify your unit › By the label

By the label

Turn the unit over. X100 units have a grey base and the label reads NMB-X100. X200 units have a white base and the label reads NMB-X200. A retired X50 has a black base.

Nimbus Docs › Identify your unit › In the app

In the app

Open Devices → your unit → About and read the Firmware line. Firmware 2.x means an X100. Firmware 3.x means an X200. If the unit is Offline, use the label method.

Nimbus Docs › How to read these guides

How to read these guides

Each guide opens with an Applies to line naming the model and firmware. Steps are numbered and must be done in order. Guides that change the state of the unit have a Before you start section that says what will be erased. Every section starts with its position in the documentation tree, so a section read on its own still tells you which product, guide and topic it belongs to.
