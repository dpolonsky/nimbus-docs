X100 Firmware 2.x Release Notes

Product: X100 · Firmware: 2.x · Status: published · Updated: 2026-10-25

2.x behavior that matters for support

  • The pairing key is preserved across updates. After updating, the unit reconnects on its own and no re-pairing is required.
  • If a customer reports needing to re-pair after a 2.x update, treat it as a fault: forget the device and pair again using Pairing (X100).
  • Updates need at least 50 percent battery or a connected USB-C supply. Below that the update aborts with E17.

Version history

  • 2.4: fixes Bluetooth reconnect delay after sleep.
  • 2.3: adds Wi-Fi channel auto-selection.
  • 2.2: security patch, no behavior change.
