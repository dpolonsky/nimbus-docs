X100 Overview

Product: X100 · Firmware: 2.x · Status: published · Updated: 2026-11-09

Nimbus Docs › Products › X100 Overview › Summary

Summary

The X100 runs firmware 2.x. It uses Bluetooth 4.2 with about 10 m range and supply 2 A over USB-C.

Nimbus Docs › Products › X100 Overview › How pairing works on the X100

How pairing works on the X100

Pairing uses a 6 digit code with a 60 second window. Firmware 2.x keeps the pairing key across updates. After an update the unit reconnects on its own and no re-pairing is needed. See X100 firmware notes and pairing troubleshooting.

What persists

Firmware 2.x keeps the pairing key across updates. After an update the unit reconnects on its own and no re-pairing is needed.

Nimbus Docs › Products › X100 Overview › Power

Power

Use the supplied 2 A USB-C adapter. A weaker supply causes E17 and E90.
