Product: X100 · Firmware: 2.x · Status: published · Updated: 2026-10-25

Connectivity

ItemValue
Bluetooth4.2, range about 10 m in open space
Wi-Fi2.4 GHz only, 802.11 b/g/n
Pairing code6 digits, 60 second window

Because the X100 has no 5 GHz radio, a router that broadcasts only on 5 GHz will never see the unit. See router band causes.

Nimbus Docs › Products › X100 Overview › X100 Specifications › Operating environment

Operating environment

0 to 40 °C, 10 to 85 percent humidity, non-condensing. Operating outside this range can cause Bluetooth disconnects that look like pairing faults.
