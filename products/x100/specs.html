Power5 V USB-C, 2 A
Status LEDGreen steady = ready, green blinking = pairing, red steady = fault

The X100 supports 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only. A router that broadcasts 5 GHz alone will never see the unit; see Wi-Fi Dropouts.
