Connectivity

Product: All · Firmware: - · Status: published · Updated: 2026-10-25

Connectivity problems fall into two groups: pairing (the app and the unit do not trust each other) and network (the unit cannot hold a Wi-Fi link).

Quick test: if the LED is on and the unit appears in your router's client list but the app says Offline, it is a pairing problem. If the unit never appears in the router list, it is a network problem.
