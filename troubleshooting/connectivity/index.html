Two kinds of connectivity problem

Pairing problems mean the app and the unit do not trust each other. Network problems mean the unit cannot hold a Wi-Fi link. They look alike (Offline in the app) but have different fixes.

Nimbus Docs › Troubleshooting › Connectivity › Quick test to tell them apart

Quick test to tell them apart

Open the router client list. If the unit appears there but the app says Offline, it is a pairing problem. If the unit never appears, or appears and disappears, it is a network problem.

Nimbus Docs › Troubleshooting › Connectivity › Decision path

Decision path

  1. Unit appears in router list, app Offline, X200 just updated: Pairing (X200).
  2. Unit appears in router list, app Offline, X100: Pairing (X100).
  3. Unit comes and goes in the router list: Wi-Fi Dropouts.
