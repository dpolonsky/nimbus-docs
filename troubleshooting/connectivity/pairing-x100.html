Pairing (X100, firmware 2.x)

Product: X100 · Firmware: 2.x · Status: published · Updated: 2026-10-25

Applies to: X100, firmware 2.x only. For X200 use Pairing (X200).

Symptoms

  • Unit is not listed in the app.
  • Unit is listed but shows Offline.
  • Pairing starts and then times out after 60 seconds.

Likely causes

  1. A stale pairing entry on the phone or on the unit.
  2. Unit is out of Bluetooth range or in sleep.
  3. Phone Bluetooth permission was revoked for the app.

Steps

  1. On the unit open Settings → Bluetooth and select Forget device for the old phone entry.
  2. On the phone, open system Bluetooth settings and forget the Nimbus X100 entry.
  3. Restart the unit: hold the power button for 5 seconds and release.
  4. On the unit open Settings → Bluetooth → Pair new device. The LED blinks green.
  5. Within 60 seconds select the unit in the app and confirm the 6 digit code on both screens.

Verify

The LED turns steady green and the app shows the unit as Online.

Still failing?

Check that the phone is within 3 m of the unit, then retry once. If it still fails, perform a factory reset from Settings → System → Reset and contact support with the firmware version from About. Firmware 2.x does not reset pairing on update, so also read X100 Firmware 2.x Release Notes.
