Wi-Fi Dropouts (X400)

Product: X400 · Firmware: 4.x · Status: published · Updated: 2026-11-09

Nimbus Docs › Troubleshooting › Connectivity › Wi-Fi Dropouts (X400) › Applies to

Applies to

X400, firmware 4.x. Wi-Fi radio: Wi-Fi 6E, tri band 2.4 / 5 / 6 GHz.

Nimbus Docs › Troubleshooting › Connectivity › Wi-Fi Dropouts (X400) › Symptoms

Symptoms

The X400 goes Offline for a few minutes at a time.

Nimbus Docs › Troubleshooting › Connectivity › Wi-Fi Dropouts (X400) › Likely causes

Likely causes

  • 2.4 GHz interference from microwaves and cordless phones.
  • Band steering moves the X400 to a band it handles poorly.
  • Router channel 12 to 14.

Nimbus Docs › Troubleshooting › Connectivity › Wi-Fi Dropouts (X400) › Steps

Steps

  1. Run Check Router Band and Channel.
  2. Keep the unit at least 1 m from other 2.4 GHz devices.
  3. Enable the 6 GHz band: Enable the 6 GHz Band.

Nimbus Docs › Troubleshooting › Connectivity › Wi-Fi Dropouts (X400) › Verify

Verify

The unit stays Online for 24 hours.
