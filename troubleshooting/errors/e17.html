Error E17: Low Battery During Update

Product: All · Firmware: - · Status: published · Updated: 2026-10-25

Nimbus Docs › Troubleshooting › Error Codes › Error E17: Low Battery During Update › Meaning

Meaning

The firmware update stopped because the battery fell below 50 percent, or power was lost during the update.

Nimbus Docs › Troubleshooting › Error Codes › Error E17: Low Battery During Update › Causes

Causes

  • Unit not on USB-C power during the update.
  • Weak adapter: X100 needs 2 A, X200 needs 3 A.
  • A faulty cable.

Nimbus Docs › Troubleshooting › Error Codes › Error E17: Low Battery During Update › Steps

Steps

  1. Connect the unit to the supplied USB-C adapter.
  2. Wait until the app shows at least 50 percent.
  3. Start the update again from Devices → About → Update. Do not unplug while the LED is amber.

Nimbus Docs › Troubleshooting › Error Codes › Error E17: Low Battery During Update › After a successful X200 update

After a successful X200 update

The X200 3.x update resets the pairing key, so expect to pair again. See the 3.x key reset and E42.

Nimbus Docs › Troubleshooting › Error Codes › Error E17: Low Battery During Update › If E17 repeats

If E17 repeats

Try another cable and wall adapter. A weak supply is the most common cause. If the unit no longer powers on, contact support.
