Error E17: Low Battery During Update

Product: All · Firmware: - · Status: published · Updated: 2026-10-25

Applies to: X100 and X200.

Meaning

The firmware update was stopped because the battery fell below 50 percent, or the unit lost power during the update.

Steps

  1. Connect the unit to its USB-C supply. Use the supplied adapter: X100 needs 2 A, X200 needs 3 A.
  2. Wait until the battery shows at least 50 percent in the app.
  3. Start the update again from Devices → About → Update. Do not unplug the unit while the LED is amber.

After a successful X200 update

The X200 3.x update resets the pairing key, so expect to pair again. See X200 Firmware 3.x Release Notes.

If E17 repeats

Try a different cable and wall adapter. A weak supply is the most common cause. If the unit no longer powers on, contact support.
