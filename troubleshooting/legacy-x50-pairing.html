Retired model

The X50 (firmware 1.x) is out of support. Hold the pairing button on the back for 3 seconds until the LED turns blue, then select Nimbus X50 in the phone Bluetooth list. Use legacy app version 1.8. This page has no inbound links on purpose: it is the negative case for reachability checks.
