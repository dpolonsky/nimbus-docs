Legacy X50 Pairing (Orphan)

Product: X50 · Firmware: 1.x · Status: published · Updated: 2026-10-25

Applies to: X50, firmware 1.x (retired).

The X50 is out of support. To pair, hold the pairing button on the back for 3 seconds until the LED turns blue, then select Nimbus X50 in the phone's Bluetooth list. The current app no longer supports the X50, so use the legacy app version 1.8.
