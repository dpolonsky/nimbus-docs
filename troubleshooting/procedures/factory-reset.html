Factory Reset Procedure

Product: X200 · Firmware: 3.x · Status: published · Updated: 2026-10-25

Hold the power button for 15 seconds until the LED blinks red, release, then pair again. Erases local settings. Pairing-related failures start at the X200 pairing page.
