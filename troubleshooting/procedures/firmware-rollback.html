Can I go back to 2.x?

No. Rollback from 3.x is not supported.

Nimbus Docs › Troubleshooting › Procedures › Firmware Rollback › Why not

Why not

3.x changes the pairing and storage format, and 2.x cannot read it. The 3.x pairing key model is also incompatible, see why the key resets.

Nimbus Docs › Troubleshooting › Procedures › Firmware Rollback › What to do instead

What to do instead

  • Pairing lost after the update is expected: Pairing (X200).
  • Unit unstable after an update: run the Factory Reset Procedure and update to the latest 3.x.
