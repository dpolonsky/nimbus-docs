Re-enable Pairing Mode

Product: X200 · Firmware: 3.x · Status: published · Updated: 2026-10-25

Applies to: X200, firmware 3.x.

Before you start

Phone Bluetooth is on, the app is signed in, and the unit is powered and within 3 m. No data is erased.

Steps

  1. Open the Nimbus app and go to Devices.
  2. Select the X200 (it shows Offline).
  3. Tap Enable pairing mode. The unit LED starts blinking white.
  4. Tap Pair and confirm the 6 digit code on both screens within 60 seconds.

Result

LED goes steady white, the unit shows Online. If the app reports E42, continue with the Factory Reset Procedure.

Context: Pairing (X200).
